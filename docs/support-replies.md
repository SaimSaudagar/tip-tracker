# Support and review replies

Ready-to-send answers for the questions Tipbook will get most. Replace anything in [brackets], keep replies short, and link the matching section of the support page (`https://<your-site>/support.html#…`).

Support emails sent from the app end with a line like `Tipbook 1.0.0 · ios 18.1 · es`: the version, the phone, and the app's language. For `es`, or any email in Spanish, use `support-replies-es.md` and the Spanish support page (`support-es.html`).

Fast, kind replies to reviews move ratings: Apple and Google both show your response under the review, and people often update their stars after one.

## Email replies

### "I lost my tips" / new phone / reinstalled

> Hi [name], I'm sorry, that's stressful. Tipbook keeps your tips only on your phone (there's no account), so they come back in one of two ways:
>
> 1. If you restored your new phone from an iCloud or Google backup, Tipbook's data comes with it. Open Tipbook after the restore finishes.
> 2. If you made a Tipbook backup file (Settings → Your data → Back up now), open Settings → Your data → Restore from a backup and pick that file.
>
> If neither applies, I'm afraid there's no copy anywhere else; we never upload your tips. From now on, a backup file once a month keeps them safe; Settings shows when you last made one. Step-by-step help: [support link]#new-phone

### How do I cancel?

> Hi [name], subscriptions are managed by [Apple / Google], so you cancel them there:
>
> - iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tipbook → Cancel Subscription
> - Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tipbook → Cancel
>
> Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. Everything you logged stays in the app on the free plan.

### Refund request

> Hi [name], refunds are handled by [Apple / Google] rather than by us, and they usually decide within a couple of days:
>
> - Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com → sign in → "Request a refund"
> - Google Play: play.google.com → Order history → find Tipbook → "Request a refund" (or ask Google within 48 hours of the purchase)
>
> If something in the app didn't work, I'd love to hear what so I can fix it.

### "I paid but Pro isn't showing"

> Hi [name], sorry about that. Please open Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same [Apple ID / Google account] you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, reply with the date of the purchase (from your receipt email) and I'll look into it.

### Import from another app didn't work

> Hi [name], thanks for trying the import. Could you tell me which app the file came from, and send a screenshot of Tipbook's import screen after picking the file? If you're happy to, a copy of the file with a few rows (you can change the amounts) helps me make Tipbook read that app's export properly.

### Bug report

> Hi [name], thanks for letting me know. To track it down, could you tell me:
>
> 1. Your phone model and its iOS or Android version
> 2. Tipbook's version (bottom of Settings)
> 3. What you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead (a screenshot helps)
>
> I'll reply as soon as it's fixed.

### Feature request

> Hi [name], thank you, that's a good idea and I've added it to the list. I can't promise when, but requests from people using Tipbook every shift are what decide what comes next.

### Taxes

> Hi [name], Tipbook keeps your records and builds the monthly employer report and the year-end summary, but I can't give tax advice. The IRS's Publication 531 (Reporting Tip Income) explains the rules, and a tax preparer can tell you what applies to you. The year-end PDF in Reports is a good thing to bring them.

## Review responses

Keep these under ~350 characters, never argue, and never ask for a better rating (both stores forbid it).

**Happy review**
> Thank you! Glad it's saving you time after shifts. If there's anything you wish it did, the email in Settings comes straight to me.

**"Too expensive" / "paywall"**
> Thanks for the honest feedback. Logging shifts, history, the calendar, monthly totals, scanning and import/export are free with no ads, and stay free. Pro adds the employer reports, insights and extra jobs. Hope the free version keeps working well for you.

**"Lost my data"**
> I'm really sorry. Tipbook stores tips only on your phone, so a phone backup or a Tipbook backup file (Settings → Your data) brings them back. Please email the address in Settings and I'll help you look.

**Bug or crash**
> Sorry about that, and thanks for flagging it. Could you email the address in Settings with your phone model and what you were doing? I'd like to fix it quickly.

**"Wrong numbers" (often overtime, tip-outs or service charges)**
> Thanks for pointing it out. Real hourly counts wages, tips after tip-outs, and service charges, divided by hours worked; overtime follows the rule set for the job (Settings → Jobs & positions). If a number still looks wrong, email me the shift and what you expected and I'll check the math.
