Tip Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The short version

Your tips, shifts and settings are stored only on your device. Tip Tracker has no accounts and no ads, and we don’t collect, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (shifts, tips, hours, wages, jobs, positions, notes and settings) is saved in a database on your phone. It is included in your phone’s own backups (iCloud or Google), which are controlled by you and your device maker, not by us.

Files you export or import

When you export a CSV or PDF, the file goes wherever you choose to send it from the share sheet. When you import a CSV, it is read on your device. We never receive these files.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple or Google. To confirm what you’ve bought, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts from the store. It does not receive your tips or shifts. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

Tip Tracker is meant for working adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new date.
