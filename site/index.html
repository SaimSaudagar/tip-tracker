Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Logging shifts, your history, the calendar, monthly totals, slip scanning, import and export are free, with no ads. Pro adds reports, insights and more than one job.

Where are my tips stored?

Only on your phone. There’s no account and no Tipbook server. Your tips are included in your phone’s regular iCloud or Google backup, and Settings can save a backup file you keep wherever you like, for moving to a new phone.

Can I bring my tips from another app?

Yes. Most tip apps can export a CSV file, usually under Settings or Reports. Pick it in Tipbook and it matches the columns by name: cash and card tips, tip-outs, hours or clock-in and clock-out times, wages and sales. You can change any match before importing.

What’s the monthly tip report?

In the US, employees who receive $20 or more in tips in a month report them to their employer by the 10th of the next month. Tipbook fills in a report for each employer from your shifts, ready to share as a PDF.

Does Tipbook do my taxes?

No. Tipbook keeps your records and makes reports from them. It isn’t tax advice; for that, ask a tax professional.

How do Pro subscriptions work?

The yearly plan starts with 14 days free, and Tipbook reminds you 2 days before the trial ends. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in your App Store or Google Play settings. Lifetime is a one-time $49.99 purchase.
