Log, change or delete a shift

  1. Tap Log shift on Home, fill in your cash tips, card tips and hours, and tap Save.
  2. To log an earlier day, open Calendar, tap the day, then tap “Log a shift for …”.
  3. To change or delete a shift, tap it on Home or in the Calendar. Delete shift is at the bottom.
  4. Tip-outs, tips from coworkers, service charges, sales, guests and start and end times are under “Tip-outs, service charges & more”.

Move to a new phone, or reinstall

  1. Your tips live only on your phone. They’re included in your phone’s own iCloud or Google backup, so restoring a phone backup brings them back.
  2. To move them yourself: Settings → Your data → Back up now, and keep the file in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email.
  3. On the new phone, install Tipbook, finish the short setup, then Settings → Your data → Restore from a backup and pick the file.
  4. Restoring replaces what’s on the new phone, so do it before logging new shifts there.

Bring your tips from another app or a spreadsheet

  1. In your old app, export your history as a CSV file. It’s usually under Settings or Reports, as Export or Backup; some apps email the file to you.
  2. From Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, save or download the sheet as CSV.
  3. In Tipbook: Settings → Your data → Import from another app (CSV), and pick the file.
  4. Check which column fills each field (tap a field to change it), look over the preview, and tap Import. Importing the same file twice won’t add shifts twice.

Monthly tip report and year-end summary

  1. Open Reports. The monthly report shows each job’s tips for the month and when they’re due to your employer.
  2. In the US, tips of $20 or more in a month from one job are reported to that employer by the 10th of the next month.
  3. Tap Share PDF for your employer to send or print it (Pro). The year-end summary has your tips, service charges and wages for tax time.
  4. Tipbook keeps records and makes reports; it isn’t tax advice.

Overtime

  1. Settings → Jobs & positions → tap a position → Overtime, and pick the rule on your paystub: over 40 hours a week (most states), over 8 hours a day (as in Alaska and Nevada), or California (adds double time after 12 hours, and the seventh-day rule).
  2. Enter the overtime rate from your paystub for exact numbers; otherwise Tipbook estimates it. Overtime applies to every position at that job.

Widgets, Siri and shortcuts

  1. iPhone and iPad: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Tipbook. It also fits the Lock Screen.
  2. Android: touch and hold the home screen, tap Widgets, and drag “Tipbook: this week” onto it. Touch and hold the Tipbook icon for Log shift and Scan slip.
  3. Say “Hey Siri, log my tips in Tipbook” to jump straight to logging a shift.

Reminders don’t show up

  1. Settings → Reminders → turn on After-shift reminder, and pick the time and the days you work. Days you’ve already logged get no reminder.
  2. Weekly recap sends your week’s totals the morning after the week ends. The amounts show in the notification, so leave it off if others see your phone.
  3. If nothing arrives, allow notifications for Tipbook in your phone’s Settings → Notifications.

Pro, subscriptions and refunds

  1. Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play, with your store account. On a new phone, tap Settings → Restore purchases.
  2. To cancel, open your subscriptions: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews.
  3. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play, not by Tipbook.

Privacy

  1. There’s no account and no Tipbook server: your tips, hours and notes stay on your phone. There are no ads.
  2. Settings → Your data → Delete all data erases everything in the app. Deleting the app does too, apart from your phone’s own backups.
